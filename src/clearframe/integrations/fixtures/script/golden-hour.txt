GOLDEN HOUR — excerpt, shooting draft 3

INT. MAYA'S KITCHEN — MORNING

MAYA (20s, restless) paces. On the table: cereal, keys, a can of
COCA-COLA sweating in the light.

                         MAYA
          If he calls, I'm not answering.

Her phone BUZZES. She stares at it. Instead of answering she thumbs
the music app — BLINDING LIGHTS blasts from the phone speaker. She
turns it up. Dances badly, defiantly.

INT. MAYA'S BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS

Posters line the wall — a framed ARCTIC MONKEYS TOUR POSTER above the
bed. DEV (20s) leans in the doorway, watching her dance.

                         DEV
          You're unwell.

                         MAYA
          Extremely.

EXT. CITY STREET — GOLDEN HOUR

They walk. The city glows. Maya finally checks her phone.
