# ClearFrame — Project Context

Autonomous rights-clearance department for film & TV. Google Cloud "Agentic Cinema" hackathon, **Parallel track**, deadline **Sep 10 2026, 2:30am IST**. Devpost judging: tech implementation, complete product (not PoC), credible impact, non-obvious idea.

## Architecture (read before editing)

Deterministic 13-stage pipeline: **script** (pre-scan) → **scan** (Gemini video, Vertex) → **triage** (rules+dedupe) → **corroborate** (independent logo catalogue confirms identity) → **drift** (script vs screen) → **preview** (complete footage-derived report, no research) → **research** (Parallel Task API fan-out, Basis citations = audit trail) → **freshness** (Parallel Search, live enforcement check) → **risk** (pure-code rubric in `scoring.py` — NEVER an LLM) → **territory** (per-jurisdiction bands) → **coverage** (rights ledger: are we already licensed?) → **remediation** (template drafts) → **court** → human review → **dossier** (E&O report + EDL/CSV markers + cue sheet). `ANALYSIS_STAGES` in `pipeline.py` is the source of truth — update it and the event-count test together.

- Core (`models.py`, `scoring.py`, `triage.py`, `pipeline.py`, `corroboration.py`, `territory.py`, `freshness.py`, `licensing.py`, `matching.py`, `routing.py`, `knowledge.py`, `audio.py`, `exporters/`) must not import `google.*`.
- **`routing.py` is the escalation ladder** — the module that decides *how much to spend* on each finding, and the reason a run is minutes rather than tens of minutes. LOCAL (rights table, 0ms/$0) → STATUTE (settled law, 0ms/$0) → SEARCH (Parallel Search, ~2s/$0.005) → DEEP (Parallel Task, minutes). A rung is only correct if it can answer the question its category poses: trademark ownership is a local lookup so SEARCH suffices, artwork ownership IS the question so art goes DEEP. Anything at or above the HIGH band escalates regardless of category — cheapest-that-can-answer is the right default, not the right rule for the findings that sink a delivery.
- **`knowledge.py` + `data/rights/`** — 151 marks, 16 cases, 18 counterparties, US term rules. Ownership is asserted locally (a corporate fact); **posture deliberately is not**, unless a documented citable action exists, because whether a holder is suing people this quarter is exactly what a static table cannot know. That is what keeps Parallel load-bearing on every material finding.
- **`audio.py`** — music identity is *measured*, not inferred. A generic label ("Upbeat electronic music") is an absence of identity, so a fingerprint hit PROMOTES it; a specific label that disagrees is CONFLICTED and blocks research as usual.
- **`use_context` and `depiction` are the two variables that actually decide trademark risk.** Rogers v. Grimaldi protects expressive works and not advertising (narrowed further by Jack Daniel's v. VIP, 2023), and the litigation record splits cleanly on it: every brand-owner *win* is an advert (Falkner v. GM, Mercedes v. the Detroit muralists, Revok v. H&M), every *loss* is an expressive work (Wham-O, Caterpillar, Louis Vuitton). Rights holders then object to *portrayal*, not presence — NBC digitally erased In-Sink-Erator from *Heroes* only because a hand got mangled in it. Both default to the calibration baseline (`EXPRESSIVE`, `depiction=None`), so a production that declares nothing scores exactly as it did before they existed. **Music is exempt from both**: sync + master are required whichever medium it is.
- **`platform.py` scores DETECTABILITY, not legal merit — and they invert.** A background mural carries real legal weight and is almost never caught; a 12-second music bed has an excellent fair-use argument and is caught essentially always, because [Content ID does not evaluate fair use](https://torrentfreak.com/youtube-processed-2-5-billion-content-id-copyright-claims-in-2025/) (2.5B claims in 2025, 99%+ automated, 90%+ monetised rather than removed). Default platform is `none` (theatrical/festival), which is honest: no automated enforcement exists there, so clearance matters *more*, not less.
- **`territory.py` answers "which law governs this finding here", not just panorama.** 12 jurisdictions × 8 dimensions in `data/jurisdictions.json`. The ones that change advice: parody (statutory in GB/DE/FR/ES, fair dealing in CA/AU, fair use in the US — **absent in India, Japan, Italy**), open vs closed exceptions (only the US is open, so a fair-use memo does not travel), incidental inclusion (India s.52(1)(u) is unusually generous *for artistic works only, not music*), moral rights (perpetual in FR/BR/ES/IT). `governing_regime()` fixes a quiet US-centrism: the same background face is GDPR in the EU, DPDP in India, LGPD in Brazil.
- **`media.py` is the only module that reads a video file directly** (ffmpeg via `imageio-ffmpeg`, no third-party service). It exists because every upload declared `duration_s = 0.0`, so `audio.sample_offsets` took ONE fingerprint sample at the head of a 49s clip instead of four across it.
- **`exposure.py` is the only module about things nobody owns.** Addresses, documents, screens, plates, minors. Kept entirely out of the clearance element list. MINOR is the ceiling in every territory and is deliberately not scaled by regime — scaling it would imply somewhere that publishing a child's face is more acceptable.
- **`conflicts.py` is the one check that is not about infringement.** Category exclusivity is standard in sponsorship contracts, so a rival mark in shot can void the fee even though the depiction is lawful. Deliberately conservative — uncatalogued marks are left alone, because a false conflict costs the user a real conversation with a sponsor.
- Identity verdicts, territory bands and coverage states are deterministic like risk scores — LLMs and detectors observe, pure code decides. A `CONFLICTED` identity blocks research; corroborator silence is `SINGLE_SOURCE`, never a conflict; coverage is `UNKNOWN` (not `NOT_COVERED`) whenever ownership or identity is unresolved.
- `scan` runs Gemini, Cloud Video Intelligence and acoustic fingerprinting **concurrently** — none depends on another's output, so running them in sequence paid their latencies added together. Results are stashed on the state; `corroborate` consumes them and falls back to calling the detector itself if the prefetch is absent.
- `<out>/state/` holds production JSON **and** `licences.json`. Never `glob("*.json")` it — use `LocalJsonStore.production_ids()`.
- Integration clients sit behind protocols with **fixture** and **live** implementations sharing one parser — demo mode (zero creds/network) exercises the identical code path. Fixtures: `src/clearframe/integrations/fixtures/`.
- ADK wrapper: `src/clearframe/adk/agents.py` (`SequentialAgent` of custom `StageAgent`s, google-adk 2.7).
- Review app: `src/clearframe/webapp/server.py` (FastAPI, server-side role gating, `asyncio.Lock` serializes all state saves) + `webapp/` React SPA (**prebuilt `webapp/dist` is committed** — rebuild with `npm run build` after any webapp/src change and commit dist).
- Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-15-clearframe-design.md`. Plans: `docs/superpowers/plans/`.

## Conventions

- TDD: failing test → implement → green → commit (one commit per task, `feat:`/`fix:`/`docs:`).
- Feature branches merged to `main` after the full suite passes twice (pre- and post-merge). `pytest` must stay green with dev-only install too (cloud-dependent tests use `pytest.importorskip`).
- Risk scores must stay reproducible from stored inputs. CSV text cells go through `exporters.safe_cell`.
- Timestamps come from callers/CLI, never inside builders (`as_of` is threaded into `licensing.assess`).
- Any change under `webapp/src/` needs `cd webapp && npm run build` and the `dist` diff committed.
- `scripts/smoke.sh` must stay green. Use its `has <needle> <curl args…>` helper for new checks — a bare `curl … | grep -q` races under `set -o pipefail` (grep exits first, curl dies of SIGPIPE 141).

## Status vs hackathon resources guide (updated 2026-08-22)

### UI-mode gotchas (found by opening the app, not by the suite)

Every one of these passed a green test suite. They live in the gap between "the
code does what I wrote" and "the thing on screen is right", and the only way
they surfaced was a human clicking.

- **`scripts/smoke.sh` used to `pkill -f "clearframe serve"`** — killing every ClearFrame server on the machine, including a dev server on another port, twice per run (the `trap cleanup EXIT` fires too). It now kills only what holds its own `$PORT`. **Never widen that pkill again.**
- **Gemini transposed the bbox axes.** The schema asked for four ANONYMOUS numbers and the prompt said `[ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax]`; the model answered x-first, the commoner convention. Word order in a prompt is not a contract — `bbox` is now an OBJECT with named edges. Diagnose box problems by drawing the stored rectangle onto a real frame with `ffmpeg drawbox`, never by reasoning.
- **One box was drawn across every appearance.** An element with four time ranges had a single rectangle, so the box measured in shot 1 was painted onto shot 4. The scan now returns `at_s` and the player draws only within 2s of it.
- **Every upload 500'd on a `NameError` that 392 tests passed over.** All footage tests bail out BEFORE the Production is constructed (409 demo / 415 type / 503 creds), so the success path — the one a user takes — was never executed. If you touch `create_production`, run an actual upload.
- **A page refresh appeared to destroy the analysis.** It never did: the pipeline is a server-side asyncio task and persists after every stage. `/api/productions` returned store order, so `demo` sorted ahead of the user's upload and the client restored the wrong one. Now newest-first with a `running` flag.
- **AudD answers HTTP 200 when it refuses.** Error 900 on an inactive account looked identical to a genuine no-match, so the dossier claimed "returned no match" for a lookup that never happened. `service_unavailable()` + `state.audio_checked` separate them.

### Live-mode gotchas (hard-won — read before debugging live mode)

- **`gcloud auth login` ≠ `gcloud auth application-default login`.** The Python SDK uses ADC only. On 2026-08-22 ADC was authenticated as a *different Google account* (`sumitgeorgian3986@`) with zero IAM on the project, while `gcloud config` showed the owner — every Vertex call 403'd with "Permission 'aiplatform.locations.list' denied". Check identity with `curl "https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/tokeninfo?access_token=$(gcloud auth application-default print-access-token)"`. Also set `gcloud auth application-default set-quota-project <PROJECT>`.
- **zsh eats `$VAR:` as a history modifier.** `"$M:generateContent"` expands to garbage (`5-pronerateContent`) and Google returns an **HTML 404 page** that reads exactly like "model not available". Always brace: `"${M}:generateContent"`.
- **Vertex REST is `v1beta1`**, not `v1` — the google-genai SDK targets v1beta1. A `v1` URL gives the same misleading HTML 404.
- **`gemini-3-pro-preview` genuinely 404s in this project** (clean JSON `NOT_FOUND` under correct auth, re-confirmed 2026-08-22). The coded fallback to `gemini-2.5-pro` is load-bearing: live runs are actually served by 2.5-pro.
- **Gemini video is the CHEAP option**: 258 tokens/second @ $1.25/M ≈ **$0.019/min**. A 90-min feature is ~$1.74/pass; Cloud Video Intelligence `LOGO_RECOGNITION` is $0.15/min (**7.8× more**). Never "optimise" the scan away from Gemini on cost grounds.
- **Parallel list prices** (docs.parallel.ai/getting-started/pricing, checked 2026-08-22): Search **$0.005/req**; Task per run lite $0.005 / base $0.010 / pro $0.100 / ultra $0.300. `planner.EST_COST` must track these — it was 10× high and Mission Control announced "$4.70" for a ~$0.40 run.
- **Live runs found five defects that no fixture could.** Re-read before trusting a green suite: a legal suffix defeated identity agreement (`{bayer,aspirin}` vs `{bayer,corporation}` = 0.5, under threshold) so a correctly identified brand was falsely CONFLICTED and blocked from research; useful articles ("Flower Vase") were sent to deep research; people labelled by role ("Father", "Grandmother") were researched while "Young Boy" correctly got a release form; music was researched from its description; and a mid-upload TCP reset killed an entire run. **No fixture could produce any of these** — they need real detector output and a real network.
- **`matching` has three matchers, not one.** `labels_match` (general), `marks_match` (brand identity — discounts legal suffixes only, so "Warner Music" ≠ "Warner Bros."), `holders_match` (rights holders — also discounts industry words, because half the music business shares them). The wrong one gives either a false conflict that blocks research on a correct answer, or a false licence match that tells a producer they are covered when they are not.
- **Retry transport failures, never deterministic ones** (`gemini_live._is_transient`). A 4xx, a safety block or an unknown model fails identically on retry, and the footage upload is not cheap.
- **`grep -q` takes SIGPIPE even from a buffered `printf`.** The smoke `has` helper was "fixed" once by buffering the body before piping — but `grep -q` stops reading at the first match, so any body over the 64KB pipe buffer still killed the writer. The dossier crossed that line and a passing check reported failure. Use a here-string (`grep -q -- "$needle" <<<"$body"`) — file-backed, no pipe, no race at any size.
- **Token-overlap matching produces confident false positives on company names.** "Kobalt Music Group" matched an owner string containing "Universal Music Group" on `{music, group}` alone — 2 of 3 tokens, over threshold — and reported a festival-only cue licence as covering a Weeknd master. `matching.holders_match` strips corporate furniture first. Being wrong in the *covered* direction is the one failure the ledger must not have.
- **ffmpeg now ships with the package** via `imageio-ffmpeg` (static binary, no brew, works on Cloud Run). Get the path with `imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe()`, never a bare `ffmpeg`.
- **AudD returns error 900 for a well-formed token on an inactive account** — a trial must be activated in the dashboard. `api_token=test` gives 10 req/day for smoke-testing.
- **`.env` needs the dot.** A file named `env` is not gitignored; it showed as untracked with the live Parallel key in it. Verify with `git check-ignore -q .env`.
